Airport & Terminal

Terminal furniture never gets a rest: 24/7 use, luggage impact, intensive cleaning. Here the technical specification matters as much as the design. We develop materials and details to the project spec, manufacture and install on site.

Send a project brief → How we work

Scope

  1. CIP / business lounge furniture
  2. Waiting-area seating systems
  3. Check-in, information and transfer desks
  4. Café, restaurant and retail areas
  5. Seating and work tables with charging points
  6. Staff and office areas

What we focus on in terminal projects

Spec compliance

Whatever standard the project calls for on fire, abrasion and durability, we select materials to meet it and add the test documents to the file.

Impact and wear

Metal frames, impact-resistant edges, replaceable seat cushions and armrests.

Integrated services

Sockets, USB, cable trays and lighting built into the furniture, with access panels always reachable for maintenance.

Night installation

Phased installation on night shifts in a live terminal, without stopping operations.
