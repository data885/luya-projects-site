How In-House Wood, Metal, CNC & Upholstery Cuts Contract Project Lead Times

Over 70% of schedule slippage in large hospitality and commercial fit-outs stems from handoff delays between separate woodshops, metal fabricators, paint booths, and upholsterers. LUYA's vertically integrated factory runs every discipline concurrently from a single CAD master file.

DisciplineMulti-Subcontractor RiskLUYA Single-Roof Execution
CNC & JoineryTolerance drift across partsDigital CNC cut-lists from 1:1 Shop Drawings
Metal FabricationMisaligned brackets & framesConcurrent wood & metal fabrication from shared CAD
Finishing BoothsBatch-to-batch stain shiftsControlled single-lot color matching
Upholstery LineWaiting on external framesImmediate inline transfer from frame to upholstery

Factory Pre-Assembly Before Dispatch

Complex reception desks, curved bars, and architectural wall units undergo dry-fit pre-assembly on our factory floor before crating, eliminating surprises during tight site handovers.

Export-Grade Crating & Room-Level Barcoding

Every component is labeled by floor and room number and packed in ISPM-15 export-ready crating for ocean or overland freight.

Frequently Asked Technical Questions

Can clients or architects inspect production in person?

Yes. We welcome factory visits for mock-up approvals and mid-production quality sign-offs.

Do you provide export crating and international freight documentation?

Yes. Export-grade packaging, packing lists, and shipping coordination are standard.

Can replacement pieces be reordered years later?

Yes. Because all CAD/CNC files and finish codes are archived per project, matching replacement items can be manufactured on demand.
