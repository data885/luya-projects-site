Fire-Retardant Standards in Contract Furniture: EN 13501-1, BS 5852 & CAL 117

Global hospitality operators, shopping mall landlords, and airport authorities require verifiable fire-reaction compliance across wood paneling, foam, upholstery fabrics, and guestroom doors. Defining the right standard at the tender stage eliminates failed inspections and costly rework.

Composite Fabric + CMHR Foam Compliance

Specifying flame-retardant fabric alone does not satisfy BS 5852 Crib 5 unless paired with CMHR (Combustion Modified High Resilience) foam. LUYA matches fabrics, interliners, and foams to each project's exact hospitality specification.

Frequently Asked Technical Questions

What does EN 13501-1 B-s1,d0 signify?

Class B indicates very limited contribution to fire, s1 stands for low smoke emission, and d0 confirms zero flaming droplets — widely required in hotel public areas and corridors.

Where is BS 5852 Crib 5 most commonly required?

It is the baseline upholstery compliance standard across the UK, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and global luxury hospitality brand standards.

Can LUYA supply low-VOC finishes and FSC-compliant boards?

Yes, we manufacture with low-VOC water-based lacquers and FSC-compliant substrates whenever specified for green building projects.
