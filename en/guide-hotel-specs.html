Hotel FF&E Technical Specification & Mock-Up Room Protocol

Preventing delays and cost overruns in 5-star hotel renovations depends on one discipline before mass production begins: complete Shop Drawings, signed-off finish sample boards, and a physical Mock-Up room. Producing both fixed millwork and loose furniture under one roof eliminates color mismatches and site handover friction.

Why Combining Fixed Millwork and Loose FF&E Matters

Splitting headboards, wardrobes, and wall paneling from seating and tables across separate workshops almost always causes wood-stain drift, mismatched bronze coatings, and staggered freight deliveries. Integrated wood, metal, and upholstery lines guarantee unified finish batches.

Fast-Track Off-Season Hotel Renovations

For operating resorts and urban business hotels with tight 45–75 day refurbishment windows, floor-sequenced crating allows parallel site installation teams to turn over wings without congestion.

Frequently Asked Technical Questions

When should the mock-up room be approved before serial production?

Approving the mock-up room at least 2–3 weeks prior to batch cutting catches electrical cutout, hardware, and veneer adjustments before they multiply across hundreds of keys.

Does LUYA prepare Shop Drawings from concept renders?

Yes. Our engineering team converts architectural concepts and layouts into production-ready AutoCAD, SolidWorks, and Revit Shop Drawings.

Can LUYA export and install outside Turkey?

Yes. We manage export crating, international logistics, and supervised site installation across Europe, the Middle East (GCC), and CIS markets.
