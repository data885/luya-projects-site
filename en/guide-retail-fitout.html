Fast-Track Retail Store Fit-Out & Night-Shift Mall Installation

Every closed trading day during a shopping mall refurbishment equals lost retail revenue. Pre-assembling display walls, cash counters, fitting rooms, and illuminated metal-and-wood fixtures to 95% completion in our factory cuts on-site fit-out schedules from weeks down to days.

Engineering Around Mall Service-Lift Constraints

Every modular fixture is engineered to fit mall service elevators, corridor turning radii, and strict 22:00–08:00 installation windows without on-site cutting.

Scalable Roll-Outs for Retail & F&B Chains

Once a flagship concept is approved, LUYA archives every parametric drawing so subsequent store openings move straight into rapid production.

Frequently Asked Technical Questions

Do you install during shopping mall night-shift windows?

Yes. Our site teams execute quiet, clean installations strictly within mall night-access schedules.

Are metal displays and integrated LED channels included?

Yes. Woodwork, powder-coated or PVD metalwork, acrylic/glass vitrines, and LED channels are produced together.

Can you adapt one flagship concept to different floorplans?

Yes. Our engineering office adapts your brand manual to each branch's exact site survey.
