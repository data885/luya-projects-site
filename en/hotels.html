Hotel & Resort

A hotel room sees hundreds of guests a year. Its furniture has to look like day one and still be easy to clean, maintain and repair. We design the typical room with you, get it approved in a mock-up room, then produce every floor in series.

Scope

  1. Headboards and bed bases
  2. Wardrobes, luggage racks, minibar units
  3. Desks, TV units, consoles
  4. Vanity units and bathroom countertops
  5. Lobby seating and reception desk
  6. Restaurant, bar and breakfast room
  7. Corridor, lift lobby and spa furniture

What we focus on in hotel projects

Mock-up room

Before serial production we build and install a complete typical room. Nothing goes into production until operations, the brand and the architect sign off.

Durability

Corner guards, edge banding, scratch-resistant finishes and removable upholstery. When a part is damaged, you replace that part, not the whole piece.

Fit for operations

Heights housekeeping can clean easily, accessible sockets and lighting points, soft-close hinges and runners.

Property schedule

Floor-by-floor delivery against the opening date. Rooms and public areas are produced in separate waves, without waiting on construction.
