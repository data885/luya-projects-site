PROCESS

From survey to installation, every step has a deliverable.

In contract furniture, the most expensive thing is a surprise. So at every step we put in writing, up front, what we deliver, who signs it off and when we move to the next stage.

  1. 01

    Survey & brief

    We listen to the space, the operating model, the budget range and the opening date. We make a first assessment from plans, a measured survey or photos.

    DeliverableScope list and roadmap
  2. 02

    Concept

    A furniture language that fits the architectural concept: form, material, colour. If there's an architect, we translate their concept into furniture; if not, we propose one.

    DeliverableConcept presentation, material palette, 3D visuals
  3. 03

    Technical drawings

    Dimensioned shop drawings, material lists and details for every piece. Coordination with the architect's and contractor's drawings.

    DeliverableSpec sheets, FF&E schedule, itemised quotation
  4. 04

    Samples & mock-up

    Samples of the selected materials, prototypes of critical pieces; a mock-up room on hotel projects.

    DeliverableApproved sample set / mock-up room
  5. 05

    Manufacturing

    Workshop production to the approved drawings. Interim inspections and photo progress reports.

    DeliverableQC records, progress report
  6. 06

    Logistics

    Packing labelled by room, floor or area. Domestic and international shipping arranged.

    DeliverableLabelled packing list
  7. 07

    Installation & handover

    On-site installation by our own crew. We stay on site until the snag and damage list is closed.

    DeliverableHandover certificate, care & maintenance guide

FAQ

We already have an architect. Do you also design?

Either works. We take the architect's concept through to technical drawings and production, or we develop the concept from scratch. Scope is set per project.

Is there a minimum project size?

A single café works, and so does a hotel with hundreds of keys. We build the team and schedule around the scale of the project.

What are the lead times?

They depend on scope, materials and quantities. We work the schedule back from the opening date together during the survey stage.

Do you take on projects abroad?

Yes. We handle shipping and installation for projects in the Gulf, the Turkic Republics and Europe.

Do you offer manufacturing only?

Yes. For projects with approved drawings or a finished FF&E specification, we quote directly for manufacturing and installation.

Let's talk about your project

Send whatever you have: plans, concept images, a room schedule. We'll define the scope together and come back with a roadmap and a quotation framework.
