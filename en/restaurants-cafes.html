Restaurant & Café

In a restaurant, furniture directly affects table turns and service flow. How many covers, where the waiters pass, how many guests the bar serves: we lay out the seating plan first and design the furniture around it.

Send a project brief → How we work

Scope

  1. Tables, chairs, bar stools
  2. Banquettes and fixed seating
  3. Bar counter and back bar
  4. Service stations and waiter cabinets
  5. Host stand
  6. Decorative panels, shelving and screens
  7. Terrace and outdoor furniture

What we focus on in restaurant projects

Seating plan

Capacity, table combinations and service aisles are drawn before the furniture. Two-tops join into fours, fours into eights.

Cleanability

Stain-resistant upholstery, wipe-clean tabletops, chair joints that don't collect dust.

Strength

Reinforced frames for chairs pulled out many times a day, table bases with levelling feet that don't wobble.

Fast opening

For operators who want to open as soon as the lease starts, we plan production in parallel with construction.
